## Videos for All-Ireland Camogie Final

The Camogie Association are offering people the oppotunity of being shown on the big screen in Croke Park on All-Ireland final day. Record youself catching a sliotar and pucking it and send it as a direct message to the [Camogie Association's Instagram account](https://www.instagram.com/officialcamogie/) to be included.
