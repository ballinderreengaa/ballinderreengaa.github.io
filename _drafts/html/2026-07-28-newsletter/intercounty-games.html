Intercounty Games

Congratulations to the Galway camogie team who defeated Kilkenny in the All-Ireland semi-final. They will play Cork in a replay of last year's All-Ireland final.

Congratulations to the Galway Masters hurling team who won the All-Ireland final against Tipperary on Saturday in Athenry.

Best of luck of to the Galway ladies football team who will play Kerry in the All-Ireland final this Sunday in Croke Park at 4:15pm. The game will be shown live on TG4.
