Ballinderren Hurling and Camogie was saddened to learn of the [passing of former GAA President, Liam O’Neill](https://www.gaa.ie/article/in-omos-liam-o-neill).

We offer our condolences to his wife Áine, children Caoimhe, Ciarán and Clíodhna and extend our sympathies to his wider family and circle of friends and admirers.
