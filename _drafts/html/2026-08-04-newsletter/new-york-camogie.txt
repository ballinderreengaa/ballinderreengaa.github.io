## New York Camogie

Congratulations to two former Ballinderreen Camogie players, Michaela and Ciara, who play with Liberty Gaels and won the 2026 New York Senior Camogie Championship yesterday.

To make the occasion even more special, the championship cup was presented by their former Ballinderreen manager Brian Molloy, now Uachtarán an Cumann Camógaíochta.
A fantastic Ballinderreen connection on such a memorable day!

Congratulations to all involved on a brilliant achievement. We are incredibly proud of you all. 💚🤍
