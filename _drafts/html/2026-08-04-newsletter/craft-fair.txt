Calling all Galway makers, crafters and food producers!

Applications are now open for the 2026 Local Food & Craft Gift Fair at the Black Box Theatre, Galway, running from 24–29 November.

Showcase your artisan foods, jewellery, ceramics, textiles, woodwork, blacksmith creations and more at one of Galway’s premier festive shopping events.

Apply by Friday, 7 August 2026

Spaces are limited, so submit your Expression of Interest today and be part of this vibrant celebration of local creativity and craftsmanship.

Apply now at https://galwayleo.submit.com/show/187
