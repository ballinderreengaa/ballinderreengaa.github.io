## Nathan Fawl

Congratulations to Nathan Fawl who finished in 3rd place in the All-Ireland U16 Poc Fada competition behind Conor Raleigh from Westmeath, who won for the second year in a row, and Damien Laverty from Antrim. He finished ahead of Ben Rooney from Waterford.
