The Glór na nÓg competition is open to members between the ages of 12 and 18. The rules are:

In 30 words max. per question:

* Who is your favourite GAA player and why?
* Imagine yourself in the future as a GAA star and you are talking to your favourite player. Think Big! Tell them how they inspired you.

### Prizes

* Grand prize: 1 club will win €10,000
* Province prizes: 4 clubs will win €5,000 each
* County prizes: 28 clubs will win €2,000 each

### How to Enter

Go to [https://www.gaa.ie/glornanog](https://www.gaa.ie/glornanog) to enter the competition.
