Premier Intermediate Championship

Congratulations to the intermediate hurling team who won their opening championship against Rahoon-Newcastle 4-21 to 1-18.

They will play Padraig Pearses in the next round who had a 1-19 to 2-14 win over Ballygar in their first game.

All-Ireland Camogie Final

Commiserations to the Galway camogie team who lost the All-Ireland final against Cork by a single point, 2-14 to 0-19.

Thank you to Ward and Burke Construction LTD who, along with Ballinderreen Hurling and Camogie Club, sponsored Fiona Ryan in the run up to the final.

Thank you to all the organisers and parents who helped make the bus trip to the final such a success.

Thank you to Connacht Camogie who provided a €500 grant towards the cost of the bus.

Glór na nÓg

The Glór na nÓg competition is open to members between the ages of 12 and 18. The rules are:

In 30 words max. per question:

  • Who is your favourite GAA player and why?
  • Imagine yourself in the future as a GAA star and you are talking to your favourite player. Think Big! Tell them how they inspired you.

Prizes

  • Grand prize: 1 club will win €10,000
  • Province prizes: 4 clubs will win €5,000 each
  • County prizes: 28 clubs will win €2,000 each

How to Enter

Go to https://www.gaa.ie/glornanog to enter the competition.

South Galway Athletics

Congratulations to Orlaith Mannion who finished 4th in her heat in the World U20 Athletics championships. She finished in a time of 13.73 seconds, 5 hundredths of a second behind Linda Botková from Czechia who qualified in 3rd place.

Galway County Local Sports Plan

Galway County Council and Galway Sports Partnership are inviting people of all ages and abilities to help shape the first Local Sports Plan for Galway County.

There will be a public consultation meeting in Gort Town Hall or Athenry Community Hall on the 26th of August between 7 and 9pm.

Online surveys can also be completed at the following links:
