## All-Ireland Camogie Final

Commiserations to the Galway camogie team who [lost the All-Ireland final against Cork by a single point](https://www.gaa.ie/article/cork-regain-the-glen-dimplex-all-ireland-senior-camogie-title), 2-14 to 0-19.
