The Architecture at the Edge Festival will take place across Galway City and County from Thursday 17th-Sunday 27th of September and they are looking for Festival volunteers.

Volunteer roles may include Gallery and Exhibition Support, welcoming visitors, providing information, event assistance and supporting artists and speakers.

More details can be found at this link: [https://bit.ly/AATEFest26](https://bit.ly/AATEFest26)
